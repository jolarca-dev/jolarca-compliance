## Requirements

1. Onboarding: security & GDPR awareness before production access;
   confidentiality agreement signed; role-based access only after completion.
2. Annual awareness refresh for all staff; role-specific modules for
   engineers (secure dev), support (DSR handling), finance (retention).
3. Phishing simulations at least semi-annually; repeat training for
   failures; results aggregated (never individual shaming) in
   `training/completion-log.md`.
4. Screening proportionate to role and lawful in LT/LV/EE.
5. Leaver process: same-day access revocation, asset return, confidentiality
   survival clauses — checklist evidence kept per leaver.
6. Contractors bound to equivalent obligations via contract.
