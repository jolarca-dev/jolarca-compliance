## Purpose

Top-level commitment to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of platform and personal data across LT/LV/EE operations.

## Scope

All systems of the Journey Of Life marketplace, all employees, contractors,
and third parties processing organizational or personal data.

## Requirements

1. ISMS established, operated, and improved per ISO 27001; scope statement in
   `certifications/iso27001/`.
2. Risk-based approach: risks live in `risk-register/`; treatment plans
   tracked to closure.
3. Roles: DPO (independent, reports to management), CISO equivalent,
   security champions per team.
4. Management review at least annually (`management-review/`), and after any
   Critical incident.
5. All subordinate policies (02–12) inherit this policy's enforcement model.
6. Continual improvement: audit findings, incident lessons, and game-day
   findings feed treatment plans with owners and due dates.

## Enforcement

Violations are handled under HR/disciplinary process; systemic gaps feed the
risk register. Exceptions only via `exceptions/`.
