## Purpose

Operationalizes GDPR compliance for the marketplace as controller (and
controller-with-processors) across LT/LV/EE.

## Requirements

1. Lawful basis documented per processing purpose (`ropa/`, `lawful-basis/`).
2. DPIA before any high-risk processing (`dpia/README.md`).
3. Data protection by design & default in product development
   (`policies/08-secure-development.md`).
4. Records of processing maintained and current (Art. 30) — `ropa/`.
5. Processor governance: DPA + TIA before engagement (`policies/07`).
6. Data subject rights honored within statutory clocks
   (`data-subject-requests/`, 30 days; DPO escalation day 21).
7. Breach assessment within 72h of awareness (`policies/06`).
8. This repository contains governance artifacts only — never raw personal data.
9. DPO is involved in all matters relating to personal data, independently,
   without instruction; contact: `docs/regulatory-contacts.md`.

## Enforcement

DPO may halt any release that violates this policy. Escalation path:
DPO → management → supervisory authority where required.
