# Seller Agreement

Marketplace commission terms and seller obligations, versioned
(`seller-agreement-v<N>.md`).

Includes:

- Commission structure & payment flow (Stripe Connected Account Agreement
  integration — sellers accept Stripe terms directly; our agreement must not
  conflict)
- KYC/VIES obligations of the seller (links to sellers-app RoPA)
- Prohibited items & content liability (DSA obligations where applicable)
- Termination, data return/erasure duties on exit

Legal counsel approval mandatory; DPO approval where seller personal data
processing changes.
