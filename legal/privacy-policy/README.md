# Privacy Policy

Versioned per language: `lt/ lv/ et/ en/`.

Naming: `privacy-v<N>.md`. Must reflect the current RoPA exactly — any RoPA
change that alters disclosed processing requires a privacy-policy review in
the same quarter.

Additional file: `diff-log.md` (this folder) — per version: what changed,
why, whether re-notification or re-consent was required, and what was done.

GDPR Art. 13/14 layering: this is the full policy; short-form notices in the
product reference it and are versioned in `lawful-basis/consent-text/`.
