# DSR Response Letter Templates

One folder per market language: `lt/ lv/ et/ en/`.

File naming: `DSR-<type>-<lang>.md`, type ∈ {access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, objection, automated-decision, refusal, extension}.

Each template must include:

1. Legal basis citation (relevant article)
2. Plain-language explanation of what is being provided/done
3. Where applicable: right to lodge a complaint with the local SA
   (`docs/regulatory-contacts.md`)
4. Approval: legal counsel + DPO sign-off lines

Templates are reviewed annually with the policy review cycle.
