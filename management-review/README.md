# Management Review (ISO 27001 cl. 9.3 / SOC 2 CC1)

Documented evidence that management directs and oversees the ISMS and the
GDPR program. Auditors treat absence of this folder as a major finding.

## Cadence & inputs

- At least **annual** full management review; quarterly DPO reports;
  extraordinary review after any Critical incident.
- Inputs: audit results, risk register status, incident & DSR SLA statistics,
  vendor review outcomes, feedback from supervisory authorities, changes in
  law/technology, status of corrective actions.

## Outputs (per session)

- Decisions & minutes signed by attendees → `minutes/YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- DPO report for the period → `dpo-reports/YYYY-QN.md`
- Action items: owner + due date, mirrored into `risk-register/treatment-plans/`

## Rules

- Minutes are finalized evidence: hashed into `audits/evidence-registry.csv`.
- Attendance of management + DPO is mandatory; absence documented.
