# Incident Register

All incidents and near-misses. Single cross-year index (SOC 2 CC7 evidence).

| ID | Detected (UTC) | Severity | Summary | Personal data? | Notified SA | Notified subjects | Status | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — |

Field rules:

- IDs: `INC-YYYY-NN`, never reused.
- "Notified SA": timestamp or "N/A — decision documented in <link>".
- Near-misses: severity `Near-miss`, mandatory short lessons column in folder.
- Closed incidents remain in the register forever; folders may be archived
  but never deleted.
